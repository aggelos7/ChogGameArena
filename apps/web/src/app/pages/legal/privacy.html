Privacy

We keep what the games need to work and nothing more. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics services, and we don't sell your data.

What we store

  • Your session: a cookie with a random sign-in token, which lasts 90 days from your last visit. We store only a hash of it.
  • Your profile: your display name, avatar choice and chosen Chog.
  • Your photo, if you upload one: we re-encode it to a small square image and keep only that, never the file you sent or its location or camera data. Removing it, or uploading another, deletes it.
  • Your wallet address, if you sign in with a wallet, and how many Chog NFTs it held when we last checked. We read NFT ownership from the public Monad blockchain.
  • Your games: rooms, matches, moves and results, and the XP, wins and achievements they earn, which other players see on the Ladder and in rooms.
  • Reports: when you report a photo, the reason and the time. Operators see how many players reported it and why, never who.
  • The terms you accepted, with the version and the time.
  • Server logs: technical details of requests, such as errors and timings. Our web server also records IP addresses; logs are rotated and kept only briefly.

Who sees what

  • Other players see your name, avatar or photo, collector badge and game results. Never your wallet address.
  • Operators of this site can see what is stored, to run it and to review reported photos.
  • Chog NFT pictures load from a public IPFS gateway, which sees your IP address as any website would. Your wallet app talks to the Monad network by itself, under its own privacy terms.

How long

  • Your data stays while your account is in use, and goes when you ask us to delete it.
  • Encrypted database backups are kept for about four weeks, so deleted data leaves them within that time.

Your choices

  • Change your name or avatar, or remove your photo, at any time on your profile.
  • To get a copy of your data, correct it or have it deleted, write to . Tell us your display name, and your wallet address if you signed in with one.
