## Key architectural decisions

- **Shape:** a modular monolith. One API, one SPA, PostgreSQL, Docker Compose on a single VM, and no Kubernetes, microservices or cloud services. `Core` has no dependencies. `Infrastructure` and `Games.*` depend on `Core`, and `Api` depends on all of them; architecture tests enforce this.
- **Authority:** the server decides everything. The player always comes from the session cookie, never from request data. Player ids are never returned to clients; members are described by display name, avatar and flags such as `isHost` and `isYou`.
- **NFTs:**
  - Every NFT ownership check that admits a player (selecting an NFT, starting a match) reads the chain live.
  - Stored ownership only backs gallery listing during an outage of up to 10 minutes.
  - An ownership outage blocks starting with an NFT; there's no silent fallback to a standard Chog (DEC-019).
- **Abilities:** `SharedAbilityPool` unless `Abilities:TraitBased:Enabled` (DEC-066).
  - Contract (DEC-032): `ChogGames.Core.Abilities`. Each game builds an `AbilityCatalog` (game type, version, definitions, one `IAbilityHandler` per ability, an `IAbilityTiming` for its activation windows); `AbilityCatalog.Create` throws on anything inconsistent. `AbilityEngine.Use` takes `{"abilityId", "target"}` (target = seat) and refuses with `not_playing`, `invalid_ability_payload`, `unknown_ability`, `invalid_target`, `ability_not_now` or the handler's own code, before any effect or draw.
  - Pool (DEC-033): `AbilityPool.For(gameType, character)` / `Offers(...)` over `IAbilityAssignmentStrategy`; `SharedAbilityPoolStrategy` returns the whole catalog and never reads the character; with the flag on DI registers `TraitBasedAbilityStrategy` over `ConfiguredTraitAbilityResolver` instead (DEC-066). `AbilityEngine`, the strategy and the pool are DI singletons; a game's abilities arrive by registering its `AbilityCatalog`.
  - Traits (DEC-034): `TraitSet` (`Core.Nfts`) and `ITraitAbilityResolver` (`Core.Abilities`) are the contract the trait-based strategy is built on (`ConfiguredTraitAbilityResolver` implements it, STORY-061). Only a strategy, the resolver and its rules may read a character's traits; `TraitAccessTests` (Architecture.Tests) scans game modules and Core's `Abilities`, `Games`, `Matches` and `Rooms` for trait access.
  - Selection (DEC-035): `RoomMember.AbilityId` is the lobby choice (`GameRoom.SelectAbility`; a different one un-readies; `DropAbility` clears it and un-readies). `LobbyService` checks the pool with `LobbyCharacters.ForAsync(player)` (stored selection, no chain call; cached traits only with trait-based abilities on) when choosing and readying, and again at start with the live snapshots; `AbilityPool.Lock` gives the `AbilitySnapshot` that `GameRoom.Start` stores in `RoomMember.Ability` (jsonb, `AbilitySnapshotConversion`). A game without a catalog needs no ability. `RoomSnapshots` shows a member's choice only to themselves while waiting (`hasAbility` to others) and the locked one to all.
  - Execution (DEC-036): `MatchCommandService` sends `USE_ABILITY` (`MatchCommand.UseAbility`) to `AbilityEngine` after the usual checks; the engine checks the locked ability (`not_your_ability`/`unknown_ability`), uses left (`ability_used_up`) and that the current handler still takes the locked values (`AbilityCatalog.Fits`, else `ability_unavailable`), then runs it with `AbilitySnapshot.ToDefinition()`. `GameRoom.ApplyAbility` counts the use in `RoomMember.AbilityUses` (check `CK_RoomMembers_AbilityUses`), keeps the turn and bumps the version. Snapshots show `usesLeft` on locked abilities and each member's `seat`. No game registers a catalog yet.
- **Chog Life rules (DEC-037):** `ChogGames.Games.ChogLife.Rules`. `LifeRules.Create` checks the board (30–40 tiles in index order, one Start first and one Finish last, forward links only, every tile reachable, only a Branch enters a Safe/Degen lane, both lanes of a fork join at one main tile, amounts only on Reward/Loss tiles, event kinds only on Event tiles) and the values (MON, bonus, cap ≤ 20 min, die faces); refusals are `ArgumentException`. `LifeRulesV1.Rules` is built once and registered by `AddChogLife()` (Api), so a bad board stops startup. `ChogLifeMatchRules` (STORY-036) plays it. `OffChainGameTests` (Architecture.Tests) scans game modules for wallets, `System.Net`, `HttpClient`, BouncyCastle and Infrastructure.
- **Chog Life board (DEC-038):** `IMatchRules.StartState(MatchStartContext)` (default null) is called by `LobbyService` through `GameRoom.Start(..., startState)` once seated, in the start's transaction. `ChogLifeMatchRules` (Games.ChogLife, registered by `AddChogLife()` over every registered `LifeRules`) stores `{rulesVersion, pawns[{seat, tile}]}`; `ViewFor` returns `{rulesVersion, tiles, pawns}` (enums as PascalCase strings, nulls left out, leavers' pawns removed, unreadable state or unknown version → null). `TimeOutTurn` is null (skip).
- **Chog Life portfolio (DEC-042):** `Rules/LifePortfolio.cs` (`Settle` applies any signed change; the match computes `lastMove.change` as the total's difference) and `Rules/LifeStake.cs` (`LifeStakeOutcome(Chance, Percent)`, `LifeLaneStake(Lane, Outcomes)`, `LifeAsset(Lane, Amount)`, and the internal `LifeChance.Pick`, shared with `LifeEvent.Draw`). `LifeRules.Create` takes optional `forkStake` and `stakes` (0/none: no staking, and the view leaves out `forkStake`/`stakeReturns`). Stored pawns add `stake`, `bonuses`, `liabilities` (zeros omitted); the view's pawns are `LifePawnView` with every part and `portfolio`. Order of draws in a `ROLL`: die, stake payout (if it rejoins the main track with a stake), then the tile's event. Web: `LifePawn` requires the portfolio fields; `stakeSummary` in `life-board-model.ts`; `lifePawn(seat, tile, mon, extra)` in `testing.ts` builds pawns for specs.
- **Chog Life abilities (DEC-044):** `LifeAbilities.Catalog(ChogLifeMatchRules)` builds catalog v1; its handlers and `IAbilityTiming` call internal methods in `ChogLifeMatchRules.Abilities.cs` (partial): `AbilityWindow` (`at_fork` while `choosing` is the player's seat, else `before_roll`; none once finished/ended/unplayable), `CanArm`/`Arm` (pawn `armed {ability, percent}`, refusal `ability_armed`), all four handlers arm (Event Preview too, valid only on a fork before its lane). `ChooseFor` with Event Preview armed calls `Previewed` (pawn `preview: [die, chance, chance]`) and sets the state's `confirming`; a second `CHOOSE_LANE` goes to `KeepOrSwitch` (switch moves the stake and drops the preview); `TimeOutTurn` keeps the lane while confirming. `Moved` (the roll's state change) replays a pawn's preview draws through `PreviewDraws`, then `Resolve(rules, tile, liquid, armed, random)` applies the armed ability. `ViewFor` adds the pawn's `armed` (id), `confirming`, and, for the viewer only, `preview` (`LifePreview { lane, roll, to, path, outcome }`, computed from the draws in the roll's order, the stake draw skipped). `AddChogLife()` registers `ChogLifeMatchRules` itself, `IMatchRules` from it, and the catalog. Web: `FORK_ABILITIES`, `USE_ABILITY`, `previewSummary`, `outcomeRule`, `signedMon` in `life-board-model.ts`; `useAbility` output on `app-life-board`; the room page sends `USE_ABILITY` with `{ abilityId }`.
- **Turn timer (DEC-045):** `Matches:TurnTimeout` defaults to 25 seconds; the room response adds `turnSeconds` (`GameRoom.TurnTimeout`, null before the start). Web: `app-turn-timer` (`rooms/turn-timer.ts`, ticks every 250 ms) above the game on the room page, driven by `turnEndsAt` and `turnSeconds`.
- **Collector cosmetics (DEC-065):** tiers are derived from `Player.ChogsHeld` at display time (never stored), so changing the milestones needs no migration. The count is written only by the gallery's ownership read. Nothing that seats, starts, plays or settles a match may read it (`CollectorAccessTests`); add any new match-deciding folder to its `MatchFolders`. Other players get only the badge, never the count.
- **Achievements (DEC-063):** `RecordStatsAsync` now requires a transaction and calls `SaveChangesAsync` itself before awarding, so the totals count the match being completed; the caller still commits. Totals: `Player.GamesPlayed`/`Wins`, distinct `GameType` of completed rooms and the clamped max numeric `Result.degenScore` (one raw SQL query, `AchievementCountsAsync`). The migration's `BackfillSql` freezes catalog v1; a later catalog version needs its own backfill migration.
- **Chog Life finish (DEC-043):** engine: `IMatchRules.TimeLimit` (default null) → `GameRoom.MatchEndsAt` (set by `Start(..., timeLimit)`, ≤ `GameRoom.MaxMatchDuration`); `GameRoom.IsDueToEnd(now)` (time up, or every player still playing `HasFinished`); `CheckCommand` returns `TimeUp` after `StaleVersion`; `ApplyCommand(..., finished)` sets `RoomMember.FinishedAt` and `NextTurn` skips finished members (all finished → the turn goes to the first with `TurnEndsAt = now`); `GameRoom.End(state, standings, now)`. `TurnTimeoutService` sweeps `TurnEndsAt <= now || MatchEndsAt <= now` and, under the lock, ends due matches first via `IMatchRules.EndMatch(MatchEndContext{TimeUp})` → `MatchEnding(State, Standings)` + `RecordStatsAsync`. Chog Life: state adds `finished` (seats in arrival order) and `ended` (`Finish`/`TimeUp`); `lastMove` gains `place`/`bonus`; view pawns gain `place`, view adds `finishBonus`/`ended`. `LifeRules.StepsToFinish(tile)` is the progress tie-break. Room response adds `matchEndsAt`. Web: `ordinal`/`timeLeft` in `life-board-model.ts`, `endsAt` input on `app-life-board` (ticks every second), the room page shows the board when `Completed` too (`data-final-board`).
- **Chog Life tiles (DEC-040):** `LifeRules.Resolve(tile, liquid, random)` returns the signed MON a tile moves (null for normal, branch, start and finish), uncapped since STORY-040 (`LifePortfolio.Settle` floors and caps it). Event outcomes are `LifeEventOutcome(Chance, Effect, Amount, Percent, Minimum)`, size `max(Minimum, Amount + liquid * Percent / 100)`; `LifeEvent.Draw` takes one `Next(0, 100)` unless there's one outcome. The die is drawn before the event. Stored pawns are `{seat, tile, mon}`; pawns without `mon` (pre-STORY-038 states) read as the starting MON, and an out-of-range `mon` is `game_state_unavailable`.
- **Chog Life balancing (DEC-047):** `LifeRulesV2.Rules` is `LifeRulesV1`'s board, events, stakes, die and cap with `FinishBonus` 50; `AddChogLife()` registers both and `ChogLifeMatchRules` starts new matches with the highest. A balance change is a new rules (or catalog, or Degen Score) version plus a rerun of the report and new tables in `docs/games/chog-life-balancing.md`.
- **Chog Life results (DEC-046):** `ChogLifeMatchRules.Results.cs` tallies `LifeStats` on the pawn in `Moved` (zero counts aren't serialized; all zeros is null) and builds `LifeResult` in `Standings`, so a roll that ends the match and `EndMatch` both carry results. The board view never shows `stats`; the web reads results from `members[].result` only. jsonb reorders keys, so compare stored results semantically in tests. EF raw SQL formats `{}`; write `'{{}}'`.
- **Chog Life forks (DEC-041):** the state adds `choosing` (the seat whose roll stopped on a branch, cleared by any accepted command) and a pawn's `lane` (set on its fork, cleared when it moves); `lastMove` gains `lane` and `byDefault`. `ChogLifeMatchRules.Apply` handles `ROLL` and `CHOOSE_LANE` (`LIFE_CHOOSE_LANE` on the web); a `choosing` left by someone not on turn is ignored. `TimeOutTurn` applies `LifeRules.DefaultLane` (a `Create` parameter, Safe or Degen; default Safe) only when the current player is choosing on a branch, else null (skip). `ViewFor` adds `choosing` (only for a player still playing) and `defaultLane`. Web: `laneTiles`/`laneSummary` in `life-board-model.ts`, the `chooseLane` output on `app-life-board`.
- **Chog Life movement (DEC-039):** `ChogLifeMatchRules.Roll` (`ROLL`) draws `Next(1, DieFaces + 1)`, moves along `LifeRules.Path(from, steps)` and returns `Accept(state, endsTurn: true)` with `lastMove {seat, roll?, from, to, path}`. A pawn on the Finish passes without a draw; a null state (pre-STORY-036 match) starts from the start tile; an unplayable state is `Reject("game_state_unavailable")`. Web: `RoomsApi.sendCommand(room, type, payload)` (new `actionId` each call; the page refetches after a command, besides the live push), `LIFE_ROLL`, the `roll` output and `rolling` input on `app-life-board`. Web: `games/chog-life/life-board-model.ts` (`toLifeBoard` validates the state, `lifeRows` lays out forks, `seatColor`) and `LifeBoardView` (`app-life-board`), shown in the room page's match section while Active.
- **Rooms:**
  - Supported games are seeded `GameDefinitions` rows; whether a game is enabled comes from `Games:Items` configuration.
  - A player can be in one open room at a time: a `Waiting` room (as host or member) or an `Active` match they haven't left.
  - Lifecycle (DEC-025): Waiting → Active (start) or Cancelled (host leaves); Active → Completed (game rules, later) or Abandoned (fewer than two players left). Every status change goes through `RoomLifecycle`; final states get `EndedAt`.
  - The room is the match: each `RoomMember` is the match player record, with its `CharacterSnapshot` (jsonb) and `LeftAt`.
  - Leaving a started match is a forfeit; the host has no special role after the start.
  - Rejoining a room changes nothing.
  - Ready is stored together with the chosen Chog.
  - The host starts once enough players are ready.
  - A host who leaves a waiting room cancels it.
- **Concurrency:** writes that must not race take Postgres row locks (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), always in the order room, then players sorted by id. The database backs the important rules with check constraints and filtered unique indexes.
- **Errors:**
  - Room APIs return problem details with a stable `code` such as `room_full` or `not_host`; clients rely on the code, never the text.
  - Unknown codes and rooms the caller isn't in both return `404 room_not_found`, so codes can't be probed.
- **Commands (DEC-026):**
  - Match commands go over REST, not the hub. Each runs under the room's row lock: replay check, then status, forfeit and `expectedVersion`, then the game's `IMatchRules`.
  - `GameRooms.Version` is bumped by every room change and is also an EF concurrency token. Game state is an opaque JSON object in `GameRooms.State`; applied actions are in `MatchActions` (PK room + action id).
  - Refusals aren't recorded. No game registers rules yet, so every command is `unknown_command`. Tests use a fake counter game.
- **Turns (DEC-027):**
  - The engine owns the turn: `GameRooms.CurrentPlayerId`, `TurnNumber`, `TurnEndsAt` and `TurnTimeout` (fixed per match at start); `RoomMembers.Seat` is the turn order (secure random since DEC-031) and `MissedTurns` the AFK count.
  - Command checks: replay, status, forfeit, `expectedVersion`, then current player (`not_your_turn`) and deadline (`turn_expired`), then the rules. `MatchRuleDecision.EndsTurn` passes the turn on; `IMatchRules.TimeOutTurn` gives the safe default (null skips).
  - `TurnTimeoutWorker` (Api, `BackgroundService`) calls `TurnTimeoutService` every `Matches:TimeoutCheckInterval`; each overdue room is re-checked under its row lock, so a turn times out exactly once. `Matches:MaxMissedTurns` (3) misses in a row forfeit.
- **Results (DEC-030):**
  - `MatchRuleDecision.Finish(state, standings)` ends the match: a rank for every player still playing (competition ranks, ties share). `GameRoom` checks them, moves to Completed and stores `RoomMember.Rank`; leavers rank after, the latest to leave highest. There is no results table.
  - `MatchResults.RecordStatsAsync` adds `GamesPlayed` for every member and `Wins` for rank 1, with atomic `ExecuteUpdate` after locking the players in id order, in the command's transaction. Abandoned matches change nothing.
  - Restart: `MatchRecoveryStartup` (an `IHostedService` registered before `TurnTimeoutWorker`) runs `MatchRecoveryService` before requests are served; each active match gets `TurnEndsAt = now + TurnTimeout` and a version bump, under its row lock.
- **Randomness (DEC-031):** all chance comes from `IRandomSource` (Core): `SecureRandomSource` (Infrastructure, `RandomNumberGenerator`) is a singleton in production, and rules get it as `MatchCommandContext.Random` / `MatchTurnContext.Random`. Payloads carry choices only; outcomes in them are ignored. `RandomSourceExtensions.Shuffle` is Fisher–Yates.
- **Deployment (DEC-049, DEC-050, DEC-051):** one VM, `infrastructure/production/docker-compose.yml`: nginx (web app + `/api` proxy over HTTPS for `PUBLIC_HOSTNAME`, the only published ports 80/443), certbot (`letsencrypt` profile; writes the `letsencrypt` and `acme-webroot` volumes, which nginx mounts read-only), the API, a one-shot `migrate` job (EF bundle, `service_completed_successfully` before the API), PostgreSQL, Redis (unused by the API), and the `backup` service (`backup` profile, DEC-051: encrypted daily `pg_dump` to the `backups` volume and an S3-compatible bucket, restore into an empty database). Secrets in a git-ignored `infrastructure/production/.env`. Images built from the repository root (`.dockerignore`), pinned base images, non-root API. nginx re-resolves `api` through Docker's DNS and repeats `proxy_set_header` in every `/api` location (a location that sets any inherits none). Runbook: `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Realtime:** one SignalR hub (`/api/hubs/rooms`, WebSockets only).
  - After each commit, `RoomNotifier` reads the room again and pushes every subscribed player still playing their own snapshot (`RoomUpdated`, the same `RoomResponse` as `GET`) (DEC-028). `SubscribeRoom` returns the current snapshot, which is how a client resyncs after a gap.
  - Clients keep the highest `version` they've seen. Recipients come from the in-memory connection registry, not SignalR groups (single instance). Hub calls have their own per-player rate limit (DEC-024).
  - Reconnect (DEC-029): the cookie is the identity; `GET /api/rooms/current` finds the open room and the shell banner links to it. The web client (`RoomLiveUpdates`) replaces a dropped connection with a new one and never gives up (0, 2, 5, 10, then every 15 s; at once on `online` or the tab shown), and shows "offline" after 3 failures. The turn timer never pauses for a disconnected player.
  - Snapshots are built by `RoomSnapshots` (Api). The game state is only shown through `IMatchRules.ViewFor`; a game without rules never shows it.
- **Rate limits:** a per-player fixed window for each feature area, configured in settings such as `Rooms:RequestsPerMinutePerPlayer`.
- **Dependencies:** SkiaSharp (with its Linux NoDependencies native assets) is the approved image library, for avatar re-encoding only (DEC-018). BouncyCastle (for keccak-256 and secp256k1) is the only approved crypto library. Wallets and NFTs use no SDKs. `@microsoft/signalr` (web) and `Microsoft.AspNetCore.SignalR.Client` (backend tests) are approved for realtime (DEC-024).

## Conventions

- **Stories:**
  - One backlog story per branch and per PR.
  - Branch `feature/story-<nnn>-<kebab-title>` from `develop`; the PR goes into `develop` with `Closes #<issue>` and the PR template.
  - Commits start with `STORY-<nnn>:`.
  - The product owner merges and then says "proceed".
- **Decisions:** open product questions are asked before coding, each with a recommended option. The answers become a `DEC-0xx` entry in `docs/decisions.md`.
- **Story status:** a story is set to `Done` in the backlog only when its acceptance criteria are verified. README sections are updated for API, config and migration changes.
- **New stories:** appended with the next free number (latest: STORY-086), placed in their epic, with a GitHub issue using the same body format, labelled `story`, `epic:E0x`, `priority:Px` and given the epic milestone.
- **Code:** problem-details errors with `code`; endpoint groups use `.RequireAuthorization().RequireRateLimiting(...)`; options are validated with `ValidateOnStart`; results are records with an outcome enum; doc comments cite requirement, story and decision ids.

## Known issues and tech debt

- **Let's Encrypt is untested end to end here:** issuance needs a public DNS name, so `deploy:verify` stands in an "issued" certificate and checks the switch; the certbot loop was checked with a stubbed `certbot`. The first real deployment should watch `logs certbot web`.
- **Backups against a real bucket are untested here:** `backup:verify` uses rclone's `local` backend as the bucket; the first real deployment should run `run --rm backup now` and `list` and check the bucket. The 03:00 trigger and the hourly retry after a failure aren't exercised by tests (start-up, `now`, `list` and `restore` are).
- **No alert on a failed backup:** failures are only logged (`ERROR`) and the hourly retry continues; alerting belongs to STORY-048.
- **The bucket key can delete:** retention needs it, so someone who takes over the VM could delete the off-VM archives; the runbook recommends bucket versioning with noncurrent versions expiring after about 30 days.
- **Restore needs an empty database:** replacing a live database means removing its `postgres-data` volume first (runbook step 2); there's no in-place restore.
- **No IPv6 inside nginx:** it listens on IPv4 in the container; Docker's port publishing forwards IPv6 clients.
- **No full Content Security Policy:** only `upgrade-insecure-requests`; a script/style policy would need the wallet providers and Angular's inline styles reviewed.
- **One database role:** the API and the `migrate` job use the same PostgreSQL user, so the API could change the schema; a separate least-privilege role would need grants and default privileges.
- **Redis has no password:** it is reachable only on the stack's internal network and unused by the API.
- **Matches started before STORY-041** have no `MatchEndsAt`, so no time cap; they end when everyone finishes (pawns already on the Finish are marked finished on their next roll).
- **Chog Race matches started before STORY-056** have no `matchEndsAt` (no cap), no arrival stamps (pawns on one tile share a rank) and statistics only from then on.
- **Chog Life is short:** engaged matches last about 2, 4 and 6 minutes for 2, 4 and 6 players (DEC-047 revised the 15–20 minute target); a longer journey would be a new story.
- **Two-player AFK abandons:** three missed turns in a row forfeit, which ends a two-player match (42% of simulated two-player matches where each player skips 30% of turns).
- **Busy fork turns:** roll, Event Preview, choose and keep share one 25-second turn; a slow player occasionally runs out (0.2% of engaged turns) and the rules take the default or previewed lane.
- **Results page:** the room page shows each member's place, a "you won" / "you finished Nth" line and, for both games, a Results panel and the final board; there's no match history list or game-specific result details (LIFE-010, STORY-043) yet.
- **Restart resume runs at every start:** including a quick restart with seconds left on a turn, which then gets a full turn. If the database is down at startup, matches aren't resumed and their turns time out as before.
- **Out-of-turn actions:** only the current player acts, or, while a reply window is open (DEC-058), only the player it waits for.
- **Turn scheduler is single instance:** like the hub registry, it runs in-process; scale-out needs one runner (e.g. an advisory lock).
- **Metrics are per process:** counters restart from zero with the API, and there's no Prometheus server or dashboard yet (DEC-052); reading `/metrics` is manual until one scrapes it.
- **Reconnects aren't counted on their own:** they show as extra room subscriptions and hub connections.
- **No dependency metrics:** Monad RPC and IPFS failures are only logged (warnings), not counted.
- **API errors aren't alerted on:** only a failing readiness check and missed backups alert; error log lines need someone to look.
- **No foreign key on `GameRooms.CurrentPlayerId`:** the domain keeps it a playing member; a composite FK to `RoomMembers` would create an insert cycle in EF.
- **Matches started before STORY-023** have no character snapshots; the room shows their current Chog, and they can still be left. Migration `AddMatchLifecycle` reclassifies a started-then-cancelled room as `Abandoned`, so rolling it back and forward is lossless.
- **No presence indicator:** other players can't see who is disconnected (DEC-029); the turn timer handles absent players.
- **Offline hint after a refused subscription:** a player who forfeited sees "still trying to reconnect" although the client has stopped; the page also says they left.
- **Started rooms in the lobby UI:** Chog Life shows its board under "The game has started"; Chog Race shows its track (STORY-050) with Roll on your turn (STORY-051), cards drawn into hands (STORY-052), the movement and chaos cards played (STORY-053), the attack and defense cards (STORY-054), the abilities (STORY-055) and the 20-minute cap with results (STORY-056). The board doesn't scroll to the moving pawn, and stays shown once the match is completed (not after an abandoned one). The illustrated board (STORY-064) is drawn in SVG over the list; the board's stylesheet was already over the 4 kB component style warning budget before it (4.5 kB, now 5.6 kB with the wide layouts; the drawing has its own, 2.1 kB). The Chog Race board's stylesheet is also over it since STORY-067 (4.8 kB, with the wide layout, arrows and cars).
- **Snapshot pushes read the room once per change:** with several quick changes each push reads the latest state, so clients may see a version skipped or the same version twice; that's harmless, since snapshots are complete.
- **A waiting-room leaver's other tabs** aren't told they left; they show the lobby until reloaded (any action answers `room_not_found`).
- **Live connections keep their identity:** a hub connection keeps the player it was opened with, even after sign-out or a wallet switch, until it reconnects. It only ever gets notices for rooms that player is in.
- **Single instance realtime:** the connection registry is in memory; scale-out needs a backplane and shared tracking.
- **Lobby display:** NFT Chogs show as `Chog #<id>` without an image.
- **Armed abilities can be wasted:** an armed Reward Boost, Loss Protection or Lucky Reroll that never meets an outcome it fits before the match ends is spent anyway (DEC-044).
- **Matches started before STORY-042** have no locked Chog Life ability, so their players have none to use.
- **No out-of-turn abilities:** abilities are used only on the player's own turn (DEC-036), never in a reply window (`reply_pending`).
- **Reply windows are a full turn:** an attack on a player holding cards waits up to `Matches:TurnTimeout` for them, even when they hold no HODL (DEC-058), so an idle target slows the race; their idling counts toward the forfeit.
- **A reply that runs out leaves the attacker's turn deadline:** a game whose reply doesn't end the turn would keep the window's deadline; Chog Race's replies always end it.
- **No foreign key on `GameRooms.ReplyingPlayerId`:** like `CurrentPlayerId`, the domain keeps it a playing member.
- **Trait rules are unmapped:** the flag ships off with no rules; the product owner still has to choose which of the collection's 12 trait types map to which abilities (and balance them) before switching it on.
- **Lobby traits can be stale:** with the flag on, the lobby uses the traits cached when the gallery last read the token; until metadata is cached (or after it changes) a holder may see options the start refuses with `ability_unavailable`, which un-readies only them.
- **Start holds a lock during chain calls:** start runs up to six live ownership checks while holding the room's row lock. That's acceptable at this scale, but worth revisiting if the chain is slow.
- **Duplicated code:** `FindCurrentPlayerAsync` is repeated in each endpoint class. (The room-lock query is now shared in `RoomLocks`.)
- **Commands return versions only:** the new state arrives as the pushed snapshot (or a `GET`).
- **Action history grows per match:** `MatchActions` keeps every applied action; there's no clean-up or retention policy yet.
- **Formatting:** Prettier isn't enforced; about 54 files differ from its formatting.
- **npm:** npm 10.9 crashes on `npm install` of new packages. Use `npx npm@11 install …` (`npm ci` is fine).
- **Mock NFT ownership** isn't exclusive: every wallet that isn't a fixture sees the same tokens.
- **Deferred:** WalletConnect (DEC-014) and smart-contract wallet (ERC-1271) sign-in.
- **No staging VM yet:** the checklist's first full pass on staging (DEC-053) is a launch gate, not done in the repo. The automated part has passed against the dev servers and inside `deploy:verify`.
- **Release smoke data:** every `release:verify` run leaves eight guests and three completed matches (one of them Chog Race, so both games must be `Available`) on the target. That’s fine on a local stack, and the reason it never runs against production.
- **Leaderboards are ranked per request:** every board call aggregates `XpAwards` (indexed by game and time); fine at this scale, a cache or materialised totals would be needed with many players.
- **XP can be farmed with extra guests:** anyone can open more guest sessions and beat them; there's no anti-farming rule (e.g. distinct wallets) yet.
- **Photo reports start over with a new upload:** reports belong to one photo; its owner can upload another (within the 5 changes per 10 minutes), which shows until it is reported in turn. Operators see only photos with reports, not who uploaded what before. Deleted photos stay in the encrypted backups for up to about four weeks.
- **Hiding isn't pushed:** a photo hidden by reports (or dismissed) changes on boards from the next room update, and browsers that already loaded it may keep showing it for up to `AvatarImages:CacheDuration`.
- **Terms and privacy are drafts:** the product owner has them reviewed legally before the launch (DEC-070); the production `.env` needs `LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL` and `MODERATION_OPERATOR_WALLETS`.
- **Photos are cached briefly:** a removed photo can still show for up to `AvatarImages:CacheDuration` (1 hour) in browsers that already loaded it.
- **Pawn photos aren't pushed on change:** a photo changed mid-match shows on the board from the next room update, since uploading doesn't push a room snapshot.
- **Pending decisions:** none; DEC-004 and DEC-005 were settled by DEC-069 (launch decisions).
